QR SCANNER / FAQ

A little clarity.

Share a link, connect a network, or discover what a code contains.

qr@haking.tr
Do my QR codes expire?

Static QR codes have no expiry. The destination website must remain available.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Images, text and Wi-Fi passwords are processed locally. Only the extension update check contacts qr.haking.tr.

Can I customize my QR code?

Choose the color and export size. PNG and SVG downloads include a four-module white quiet zone for reliable scanning.

Which browsers are supported?

Use the website in modern browsers. Official Chrome and Edge store listings are available. Chromium ZIPs can be installed manually. Firefox needs signing for permanent installation; Safari currently uses the web tools.
