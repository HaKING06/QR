QR Scanner is an open-source project by HaKING (Halit). Built to make everyday QR tasks fast, accessible and private. Create website, text, Wi-Fi and email QR codes, or read a code from an image.

Private by design

QR contents are never sent to our servers. No analytics, tracking cookies or QR history. Theme and language preferences stay on your device. Hosting providers may process standard connection logs.

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera: Chromium package. Firefox: separate package. Safari requires an Apple build.

Edge store version may differ. ZIP packages contain v1.1.1. Firefox ZIP is for temporary testing until signed.
