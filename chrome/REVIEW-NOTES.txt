QR Scanner — review/testing notes

No account, login, subscription or test credentials are required.

1. Open the extension from Chrome's toolbar on a normal HTTP(S) website tab.
2. Select Create QR, choose Website and enter https://qr.haking.tr/.
3. Generate the QR and download PNG or SVG. Copy image uses the clipboard
   only when the user clicks its button and the browser allows it.
4. Select Read QR and upload/drop/paste the generated PNG. The decoded
   value should be https://qr.haking.tr/.
5. Scan current tab uses activeTab granted by the user's toolbar action.
   Chrome internal pages, the Web Store and restricted pages cannot be captured.
6. QR for this tab creates a code for the active HTTP(S) tab URL.
7. Language and theme menus support eight languages (including Russian and Arabic RTL) and light/dark/system modes.
8. Upload an image with several QR codes; choose a result or copy all decoded values.
9. The optional bell requests notifications only on a user click. storage keeps preferences and update deduplication state; no QR history is stored.
10. On an installed-version increase, release notes open once. Same-version reloads do not reopen them. Review prompts can be dismissed; support payments are entirely optional.

Images, screenshots and QR contents are processed locally. No content scripts
are injected and browsing history is not monitored. The sole remote release
request fetches https://qr.haking.tr/version.json as data, never as executable
code. A newer-version notice appears only when its version is greater than
the installed manifest version. Camera scanning belongs to the companion
website; no camera permission is requested by the extension. A localized welcome/release page on qr.haking.tr opens after install/update and receives only version/source metadata.
