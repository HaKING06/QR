QR Scanner reads codes from images and, at your request, from the visible part of the current tab. Upload an image, drag it into the extension or paste it from the clipboard. When an image contains several codes, you can choose a result and copy the decoded text.

Create a code to share a link or your own text. You can also prepare one to share Wi-Fi access details or a contact card. Choose the color and output size, then download the result as PNG or SVG. A separate button creates a code from the current tab's address.

Images and code contents are processed on your device, are not sent to our servers and are not kept in a QR history. Language and theme preferences are stored locally. The extension contacts qr.haking.tr for release information without sending code contents or the current tab's address. Desktop update notifications are used only if you enable them.
