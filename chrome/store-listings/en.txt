Name: QR Scanner

Summary / Short description:
Read images, scan the current tab and create QR codes without leaving your browser.

Detailed description:
QR Scanner lets you read and create QR codes without leaving your browser. Choose an image, drag it in or paste a screenshot to decode its contents on your device. Create a code from your own content, choose its color and output size, and download it as PNG or SVG.

Website, Text, Wi-Fi, Email, Phone, SMS, Contact card.

• QR for this tab
• Scan current tab
• Find multiple QR codes in one image and choose the result you need.
• Eight languages, including Russian and Arabic with right-to-left layout.
• Clear release notes after a real extension update, with optional desktop notifications.
• PNG for sharing. SVG for sharp prints at any size.
• Static QR codes. No expiry and no redirect service.
• Completely free

QR contents are never sent to our servers. No analytics, tracking cookies or QR history. Theme and language preferences stay on your device. Hosting providers may process standard connection logs.
